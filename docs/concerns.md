- When joining a room, players who aren't logged in should be able to provide a name instead of being identified as "Guest". Providing a name should be optional.
- In Yahtzee, add undo functionality, including for the room version. The feature is not always enabled. On the game setup page, the host can turn it on. Undo only works on your own turn. You can undo dice rolls, not score selection. The reasoning is that someone might select the wrong dice, roll, and then realize they selected the wrong dice or forgot to select one. They can undo the roll, reselect the dice, and roll again. You cannot undo an incorrect score selection, since the turn will pass to the next player by the time you want to undo. So, if you roll a yahtzee and accidentally select chance, you can't fix that. For that reason, each score selection, there should be a confirmation popup, where you confirm your score selection. So if you tap or click to assign your roll to something, like "Full House," a popup will appear asking you to confirm that you want to score your dice as a full house.
- In Yahtzee, instead of a separate score panel, show scores for all players on the main screen and ensure that horizontal scrolling works well.
- In Yahtzee, during other players' turns, show the options they can choose and the possible scores for each option, as you would on your own turn. Display recorded scores in a different color from the available options.
- Clear the Deck name should have "Tens" in parentheses on the home and room pages, like "Clear the Deck (Tens)" and anywhere else it shows up
- In Clear the Deck, the "Back to Room" button on the right side of the top bar touches the right edge of the screen on mobile. There should be padding on the right. This may also affect other games.
- I ended a room while a game was in progress, and a 500 error briefly flashed on the screen before disappearing. I'm not sure what caused it, but errors should not flash on the screen, especially when they do not affect anything.
- Error pages, including 404 pages, need to use the current color scheme and include a link to return to the home page.
